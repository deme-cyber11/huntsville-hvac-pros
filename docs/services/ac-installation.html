AC Installation & Replacement in Huntsville, AL — Sized Right, Built to Last

Replacing an air conditioning system is the largest HVAC investment most Huntsville homeowners make. A system sized incorrectly for your home's square footage and insulation will underperform and fail early — it doesn't matter how good the brand is. Proper installation means a Manual J load calculation, correct refrigerant line sizing, and commissioning to manufacturer specs. Alabama's long cooling season makes the margin for error small.

When It's Time to Replace Your AC

  • System is 15+ years old and breaking down during peak summer heat
  • R-22 refrigerant system — R-22 is phased out, replacement refrigerant costs have made repeated repairs uneconomical
  • Repair cost exceeds 50% of replacement value — compressor or coil failures on aging equipment
  • Can't maintain temperature on 95°F+ days despite recent service calls
  • Electric bills climbing year-over-year without any change in usage habits
  • Refrigerant leak requiring more than one recharge per season — the leak is never really fixed, just refilled

How AC Installation Works

  1. Load calculation: We size the system to your home — square footage, ceiling height, insulation, window exposure. Guessing tonnage causes short cycling and early compressor failure.
  2. Equipment selection: We present 2–3 options across price points, efficiency ratings (SEER2), and brands. You choose with real cost and performance information in hand.
  3. Installation: Indoor air handler, outdoor condenser unit, refrigerant lines, electrical connections, and condensate drainage — all per manufacturer specs and local code.
  4. Commissioning & walkthrough: We verify refrigerant charge, airflow balance, and thermostat calibration. You get a system walkthrough and full written documentation.

What Every AC Installation Includes

  • Manual J load calculation — not an estimate, not a guess
  • Equipment delivery and old unit removal
  • Indoor + outdoor unit installation per manufacturer specs
  • Refrigerant line set and all electrical connections
  • Condensate drain installation and flow test
  • Thermostat installation and calibration
  • Written warranty documentation — equipment and labor
  • Permit pulled if required by municipality

What Does AC Installation Cost?

A full AC system replacement in the Huntsville area typically runs $3,500–$7,500 installed, depending on system size (tonnage), efficiency rating (SEER2), and brand. A full HVAC replacement — new AC unit plus gas furnace or air handler — runs $7,000–$14,000. The installed price covers everything: equipment, labor, refrigerant, and standard electrical. We provide written estimates before any commitment — the price you approve is the price you pay.

Common Questions About AC Installation

What size AC do I need for my Huntsville home?

It depends on square footage, insulation quality, ceiling height, and window count — not square footage alone. A 2,000 sq ft home typically needs a 3–4 ton system, but it varies. We run a Manual J calculation before recommending any equipment. An oversized system short-cycles and wears out faster; undersized can't keep up on the hottest Alabama days.

How long does AC installation take?

Most residential replacements are completed in one day. New construction or first-time installations that require ductwork may take 2–3 days. We'll give you a realistic timeline upfront.

What brands do you install?

We install all major brands — Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, and others. We'll recommend what makes sense for your budget and efficiency goals, not just what has the highest margin for us.

Do I need to be home during installation?

Yes — someone needs to be available to confirm equipment placement, thermostat location, and to receive the system walkthrough at completion. Most installations wrap up in a single day.
