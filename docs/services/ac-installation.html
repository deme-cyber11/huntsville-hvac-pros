When It Is Time to Replace Your AC

Air conditioning systems in Huntsville work harder than systems in most of the country. Alabama's 95-degree summers with oppressive humidity push compressors to the edge of their design limits, which means systems that might last 18 years in a mild climate often start failing at 12 to 15 years here.

If your system is over 10 years old and you are facing a major repair -- compressor, evaporator coil, or repeated component failures -- it is worth comparing repair cost against replacement cost. A new system will cool your home better, run more efficiently, and come with a full manufacturer warranty.

How It Works

Our Installation Process

A proper AC installation is not just swapping boxes. It starts with understanding your home and ends with verifying performance.

1

In-home assessment

We evaluate your home's square footage, insulation, window exposure, ductwork condition, and existing system. No guesswork.

2

Manual J load calculation

We calculate exactly how much cooling capacity your home needs. An oversized system cycles too frequently and fails to dehumidify. An undersized system runs constantly without reaching temperature. Both cost you money and comfort.

3

System selection

Based on the load calculation, we recommend equipment options at different efficiency levels and price points. You choose what fits your budget and goals.

4

Professional installation

Our licensed technicians install the system to manufacturer specifications. Proper refrigerant charge, correct airflow, sealed connections, and verified ductwork.

5

Commissioning and walkthrough

We test the system, verify temperature differential and airflow at every register, and walk you through your new thermostat and maintenance schedule.

Energy Efficiency and SEER Ratings

SEER (Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio) measures how efficiently an AC system cools over an entire season. Higher SEER means lower operating costs. In Alabama, the minimum required SEER rating for new installations is 15.

For Huntsville homes, we typically recommend 16 to 20 SEER systems. We break down the numbers for you: equipment cost, projected annual savings, and payback period so you can see exactly when the investment pays for itself.
