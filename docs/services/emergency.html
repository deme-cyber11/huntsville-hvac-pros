Emergency HVAC Service in Huntsville, AL — Same-Day Response, 24/7

HVAC emergencies don't respect business hours. When your AC fails at midnight during a July heat wave, or your heat goes out before a winter cold front, the situation is urgent. Emergency service means a technician responds within hours, diagnoses the failure, and makes the repair in the same visit when parts are available. North Alabama's extreme summer humidity makes AC failure a health issue — not just a comfort problem — especially for households with elderly family members, young children, or medical conditions.

What Qualifies as an HVAC Emergency

  • Complete loss of cooling when outdoor temps are 85°F+ — especially with vulnerable household members
  • Complete loss of heating when indoor temperatures are dropping toward 50°F or below
  • Burning or electrical smell from the HVAC unit — shut it off and call immediately
  • Gas smell near a gas furnace — leave the house immediately, call your gas company first, then us
  • Carbon monoxide detector alarm with HVAC running — evacuate, call 911, then call us
  • System trips the breaker every time it runs — do not keep resetting it; compressor damage compounds quickly
  • Refrigerant leak with visible ice buildup or hissing sound — system should be shut off until diagnosed
Gas smell or CO alarm? Evacuate immediately. Call your gas company or 911 first. Do not re-enter until cleared. Then call us at (256) 215-9287.

How Emergency Service Works

  1. Call (256) 215-9287: We answer or return your call within 30 minutes. Describe the symptom — we'll give you an honest first read on what it might be and a realistic ETA.
  2. Same-day dispatch: We target 2–4 hours for Huntsville area emergency calls. Real time windows, not vague "sometime today" estimates.
  3. On-site diagnostic: We diagnose the failure, show you the affected component, and provide a written repair quote before any work begins.
  4. Same-visit repair: We stock common emergency parts — capacitors, contactors, thermostats, fan motors — on the service van. Most common failures are fixed in the same visit.

What Every Emergency Call Includes

  • Same-day response within 2–4 hours for Huntsville area
  • Full system diagnostic on arrival — not just the obvious symptom

  • Same-visit repair when parts are available on the van

What Does Emergency Service Cost?

Emergency service calls typically carry a diagnostic fee of $100–$150, credited toward the repair if you proceed. Repair cost depends on what failed — most common emergency repairs (capacitor, contactor, fan motor, thermostat) run $150–$600. After-hours and weekend calls may carry a higher diagnostic fee — we'll be clear about cost on the phone before you commit. The price you approve in writing is the price you pay.

Common Questions About Emergency HVAC Service

How fast can you actually respond?

We target 2–4 hours for Huntsville area emergency calls. During peak summer, every tech may already be dispatched. We'll give you an honest ETA when you call — not a number we can't deliver on. If we're fully committed and can't get to you in time, we'll tell you that too.

Do you charge extra for after-hours service?

After-hours and weekend calls typically carry a higher diagnostic fee than standard business-hours calls. We'll be upfront about the difference when you call, before you commit to the service call. No surprises on the invoice.

What should I do while waiting for the technician?

Cooling failure in summer: close blinds on sun-facing windows, move to the coolest room in the house, use fans to circulate air, check that the thermostat is set correctly and the air filter isn't completely blocked. Heating failure in winter: close off unused rooms to retain heat in occupied spaces, use safe supplemental heat if available, and keep an eye on indoor temperature if you have vulnerable household members.

My AC tripped the breaker — should I keep resetting it?

No. A single trip could be a one-time event. If it trips again when you reset and run the system, that indicates a fault — compressor, capacitor, or wiring. Repeated resets can cause compressor damage that turns a $300 repair into a $1,500+ replacement. Describe the symptom when you call and we'll advise.
