Common Emergencies We Handle

Our emergency technicians are equipped and experienced to handle the full range of HVAC failures:

Compressor failure

the most common cause of complete AC shutdown in summer

Blown capacitor

prevents the compressor or fan motor from starting. Quick fix if caught early.

Refrigerant leak

system runs but produces warm air. Can be recharged temporarily while the leak is located and repaired.

Ignition failure

furnace will not light. Could be a faulty igniter, flame sensor, or gas valve.

Blower motor failure

system heats or cools but cannot push air through the ducts

Thermostat failure

system will not respond to commands. Sometimes the simplest fix -- sometimes a wiring issue.
