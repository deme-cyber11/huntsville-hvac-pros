When Your AC Fails, Every Minute Matters

Alabama summers average 95 degrees or higher with suffocating humidity. When your AC stops cooling, interior temperatures climb fast — 5 to 10 degrees per hour in a sealed home. For elderly family members, young children, and pets, it becomes a health risk within hours.

Most AC failures come down to a handful of common components. A capacitor burns out. A refrigerant line develops a slow leak. A contactor fails. These are all fixable — same day, in most cases — if you call before the problem cascades into something bigger.

Waiting is what turns a $200 repair into a $2,500 compressor replacement.

What We Repair

Every component, same-day diagnosis, written quote before any work begins.

Compressors

The heart of your AC. When it fails, nothing cools. We diagnose repair vs. replace and give you honest numbers on both.

Capacitors & Contactors

The most common AC failure in Huntsville. A bad capacitor prevents your compressor or fan motor from starting. Typical repair: $150–$300.

Refrigerant Leaks

If your system is low on charge, it is leaking. We find it, fix it, and recharge to manufacturer specs. No guesswork, no band-aid recharges.

Fan Motors

Condenser and blower fan motors wear out, especially in Alabama's heat. Grinding or squealing sounds are the early warning — don't ignore them.

Thermostats

Sometimes the problem is not the AC at all. Faulty thermostats account for more service calls than most people expect. Quick fix, big difference.

Ductwork

Leaking ducts waste 20–30% of your conditioned air. If some rooms cool fine and others don't, ductwork is the first place to check.
