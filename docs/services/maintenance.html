Seasonal Tips for Huntsville Homeowners

Between professional tune-ups, there are things you can do to keep your system running well:

Change your filter every 1 to 3 months

In Huntsville's pollen season (March through May), monthly changes make a real difference.

Keep your outdoor unit clear

Trim vegetation back at least 2 feet from the condenser. Clear leaves, grass clippings, and debris from the unit regularly.

Do not close too many vents

Closing more than 20 percent of your vents creates pressure imbalances that stress the system and reduce efficiency.

Check your thermostat settings before each season

Switch from heating to cooling mode in spring, and back in fall. Consider a programmable thermostat if you do not have one -- the energy savings pay for the thermostat within a year.

Listen for changes

New sounds, reduced airflow, or rooms that are not reaching temperature are all early warning signs worth a service call before they become emergency repairs.
