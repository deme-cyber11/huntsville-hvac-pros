About Huntsville HVAC Pros

Huntsville HVAC Pros isn't just another service company; we're your neighbors, deeply rooted in the heart of Huntsville, AL. Founded in 2008 by Mark and Susan Thompson, long-time residents themselves, our journey began with a simple yet profound realization: Huntsville deserved an HVAC service that combined technical excellence with genuine community care. Having experienced firsthand the unpredictable Alabama weather, from humid summers to surprising winter chills, we understood the critical importance of a reliable heating and cooling system. Our vision was to create a company built on trust, transparency, and an unwavering commitment to the comfort of our fellow Alabamians. We started small, with a single van and a big dream, growing through word-of-mouth thanks to the incredible support of the Huntsville community. Our passion is rooted in ensuring every family in Rocket City enjoys a perfectly comfortable home, creating safe and healthy indoor environments year-round. Today, our dedication to serving this vibrant city remains as strong as ever, a testament to our authentic local roots and our pledge to keep Huntsville comfortable, one home at a time.
