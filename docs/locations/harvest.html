Common HVAC Needs in Harvest

  • New construction dust -- Builder-grade filters in new Harvest homes clog faster than expected due to construction dust in ductwork. Early maintenance prevents premature wear on new systems
  • Seasonal strain -- Alabama's cooling season runs April through October, putting more hours on your system than most manufacturers anticipate. Spring tune-ups before peak season are critical
  • Aging equipment -- Older Harvest homes with 12-15+ year old systems face increasing repair frequency. We help you decide when repair makes sense and when replacement is the smarter investment
  • Efficiency upgrades -- Upgrading from older equipment to modern high-SEER systems can significantly reduce monthly energy costs

Seasonal Maintenance Matters

Harvest homeowners who schedule regular maintenance catch problems before they become emergencies. A $89-$149 tune-up that identifies a worn capacitor or low refrigerant prevents the $500-$2,500 repair that happens when those small issues cascade into major failures during peak summer.
