Waiting Costs More Than Just Comfort

  • In Alabama's humid summer heat, interior temperatures in an unconditioned home can rise 5–10°F per hour. Established Jones Valley homes often have less insulation than newer construction, making this faster.
  • Older systems common in Jones Valley are more vulnerable to cascading failures — a weak capacitor doesn't just fail in isolation, it stresses the compressor while it's declining.
  • Calling when symptoms appear — reduced cooling, longer run times, unusual sounds — gives you repair options. Calling at complete failure means emergency pricing with no leverage.
  • Duct systems in older Jones Valley homes sometimes have air leaks that reduce efficiency significantly. A tune-up often identifies these as part of the inspection.
