Why Waiting Costs More Than Comfort

  • Health risk in extreme heat — In 95°F+ conditions, interior temperatures in an unconditioned home can rise 5–10°F per hour. For households with elderly family, young children, or pets, this is a health issue.
  • Older homes, higher risk — Scottsboro has a mix of older established homes and newer subdivisions — older systems are more prone to cascading failures when a minor issue is ignored for a season.
  • Small repairs become big ones — A failing capacitor ($150–$300 repair) that goes unfixed stresses the compressor until it fails ($1,500–$2,500 replacement).
  • Early symptoms = more options — Calling at first symptoms gives you options. Waiting for full breakdown means emergency rates and no leverage.
