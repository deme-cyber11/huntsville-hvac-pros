AC repair costs in Huntsville typically range from $150 to $500 for common repairs like capacitor replacement, contactor replacement, or refrigerant recharge. Compressor replacement can run $800 to $2,000+ depending on the unit. We always provide a written diagnosis and price before starting any work. You decide whether to proceed.

Use the "5,000 rule": multiply the repair cost by the age of your unit in years. If the result exceeds $5,000, replacement is usually the smarter investment. Also consider: if your system is over 15 years old, uses R-22 refrigerant (now discontinued), or has had multiple major repairs in the past 3 years, replacement is almost always the right call. We give you both options with honest numbers so you can decide.

In Alabama's hot, humid climate, proper sizing is critical. As a rough guide, you need about 1 ton of cooling per 400 to 600 sq ft, but a Manual J load calculation is the only accurate way to size a system. It accounts for insulation, windows, ceiling height, and sun exposure. An oversized AC will not dehumidify properly (a major issue in Alabama), and an undersized system will run constantly and fail early. We perform this calculation on every installation.

We recommend two professional tune-ups per year: one in spring before summer AC season and one in fall before heating season. Alabama's long, hot summers put extreme stress on AC systems. Regular maintenance extends system life by 3 to 5 years and helps you avoid emergency breakdowns when temperatures hit 95 degrees.
