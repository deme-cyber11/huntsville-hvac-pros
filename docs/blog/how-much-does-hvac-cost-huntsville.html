How Much Does HVAC Service Cost in Huntsville?

January 20, 2026 · 8 min read

One of the most common questions Huntsville homeowners ask before calling an HVAC company is: how much is this going to cost? It is a fair question, and one that too many companies avoid answering directly. Here is a straightforward look at real HVAC pricing in the Huntsville area, based on the work we do every day.

AC Repair Costs: $150 to $500

Most AC repairs in Huntsville fall in the $150 to $500 range. The specific cost depends on what failed and how long the repair takes. Here are the most common repair jobs and their typical price ranges:

  • Capacitor replacement: $150 to $300. This is the single most common AC repair in Huntsville. Capacitors fail frequently in our climate because of the sustained heat and electrical load during summer. The part itself costs $10 to $30, but you are paying for the diagnostic, labor, and warranty.
  • Contactor replacement: $150 to $350. Contactors are electrical switches that control the compressor and condenser fan. They wear out over time and are a routine repair.
  • Fan motor replacement: $250 to $500. Condenser fan motors and blower motors both fail with age. The condenser fan motor sits outside and takes the brunt of the weather.
  • Refrigerant recharge: $200 to $600. The cost depends on the type of refrigerant and the amount needed. R-410A is the current standard. If your system uses the older R-22, expect higher costs because R-22 has been phased out and is now expensive.
  • Thermostat replacement: $150 to $350 installed. Includes the thermostat, wiring, and programming.

We always provide a written diagnosis and price before starting any repair. The diagnostic fee, typically $75 to $150, is credited toward the repair if you proceed.

AC Installation Costs: $4,500 to $12,000

A complete AC system replacement in Huntsville typically costs between $4,500 and $12,000 installed. The wide range reflects differences in system size, efficiency, and installation complexity.

  • Budget systems (14-15 SEER2): $4,500 to $7,000. Brands like Goodman and certain Carrier models offer solid performance at a lower price point. These systems meet current efficiency standards and come with manufacturer warranties.
  • Mid-range systems (16-18 SEER2): $6,000 to $9,000. This is the sweet spot for most Huntsville homeowners. Better efficiency means lower monthly energy bills, and the payback period on the additional investment is typically 4 to 6 years.
  • Premium systems (19+ SEER2): $8,000 to $12,000. Variable-speed compressors, advanced humidity control, and the lowest operating costs. Best for homeowners who plan to stay in their home long-term.

These prices include equipment, labor, permits, refrigerant, and removal of the old system. They assume existing ductwork is in good condition. If ductwork needs repair or replacement, add $1,000 to $3,000 to the total.

Heating System Costs

Heating system costs in Huntsville depend on the type of system you need.

  • Gas furnace replacement: $2,500 to $6,000 installed. Gas furnaces are efficient and provide fast, powerful heat. Most Huntsville homes with natural gas connections use a gas furnace paired with a central AC unit.
  • Heat pump system: $4,500 to $10,000 installed. Heat pumps handle both heating and cooling, making them efficient for Alabama's climate. They cost more upfront than a standalone furnace but eliminate the need for a separate AC unit.
  • Furnace repair: $150 to $600. Common repairs include igniter replacement ($150 to $300), blower motor replacement ($300 to $600), and flame sensor cleaning ($100 to $200).

Maintenance Plan Costs: $149 to $249 per Year

Annual maintenance plans in Huntsville typically cost between $149 and $249 per year. Most plans include two visits: one in spring for your AC and one in fall for your heating system. Here is what a typical maintenance visit includes:

  • Full system inspection and performance test
  • Refrigerant level check
  • Electrical component testing
  • Coil cleaning
  • Drain line clearing
  • Filter replacement
  • Thermostat calibration

Many plans also include priority scheduling, discounts on repairs (typically 10 to 15 percent), and waived diagnostic fees. For a system that runs as hard as Huntsville AC units do, maintenance plans typically pay for themselves by preventing one repair per year that would have cost more than the plan itself.

What Affects HVAC Pricing

Several factors influence what you will pay for HVAC service in Huntsville:

  • System age: Older systems often require more expensive repairs because parts are harder to source. Systems over 15 years old may not have replacement parts available at all.
  • System size: Larger homes require larger systems, which cost more to purchase and install. A 5-ton system costs significantly more than a 2.5-ton system.
  • Accessibility: Systems installed in tight attics, crawlspaces, or difficult-to-reach locations take longer to service, increasing labor costs.
  • Time of year: Emergency calls during peak summer or winter cost more than scheduled service during shoulder seasons. Planning ahead saves money.
  • Ductwork condition: Leaky, damaged, or undersized ductwork reduces system performance and may need repair or replacement during installation.

How to Get the Best Value

Getting the best value on HVAC service does not mean finding the cheapest price. It means paying a fair price for work that is done correctly. Here are practical tips:

  • Get multiple quotes: For any job over $500, get at least two or three quotes. Compare total installed costs, not just equipment prices.
  • Verify licensing: Alabama requires HVAC contractors to be licensed through the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors. Ask to see the license before hiring.
  • Ask for a written quote: Any reputable company will provide a written diagnosis and price before starting work. Walk away from anyone who will not.
  • Invest in maintenance: Regular maintenance extends system life, reduces repair frequency, and keeps energy bills lower. It is the single best return on investment in HVAC.
  • Do not defer small repairs: A $200 repair today prevents a $2,000 repair next summer. Waiting is almost always more expensive in the long run.
